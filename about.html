01 / Profile

Who is TF?

I’m Jisu Kim (TF), a student in the Department of Cybersecurity at Ajou University. I’m interested in how web applications and cloud environments fail — and how to make them fail less. Right now I’m focused on web and cloud security, and I plan to branch out into more fields.

This archive is where I keep what I learn: CTF write-ups, bug bounty notes, paper summaries and the occasional build log. Writing things down is how I make sure I actually understood them.

Outside of security, I like dark cities, rainy nights and rooftops with a good view.
